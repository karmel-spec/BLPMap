PHOTO SHOT CARD · SQUARE FORMAT

UPRIGHT — 13 SHOTS
  • 1 Main straight front, keys showing
  • 2-3 Entire piano, each side
  • 4 Music desk / panel detail
  • 5 Music desk, plate & pins
  • 6 Corner arm, cheek block, keyslip
  • 7 Decal, keys, hardware, pedals
  • 8 Toe block, caster, kneeboard
  • 9-13 Interior: trapwork · plate corner · hammer line · action · top-down inside
GRAND — 13 SHOTS
  • 1 Plate reflection on lid (dream) or open-lid front
  • 2-3 Entire piano, each side
  • 4 Music desk, plate & pins
  • 5 Corner arm, cheek block, keys
  • 6 Keyslip, felt, hardware, decal
  • 7 Lyre & pedals
  • 8 Leg & caster
  • 9-13 Interior: plate corner · hammer line · bass strings · pins top-down · full plate angled
