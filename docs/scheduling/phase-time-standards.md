Derived 2026-07-27 from 2,540 classified calendar events (2026-01-01→07-27,
19 calendars via gog -a karmel@). Scheduled hours, not clocked — planning
standards. Median hours per piano unless noted:

- **CAP**: expert (Courtney) ~35h; trainee ~60–80h (multiplier ≈2×)
- **PRSB**: trainee ~37–40h per piano; experts (Curtis/Matthew/McKinly,
  ~1,060h capacity) calendar bare "PRSB" blocks with NO serial — expert
  per-piano standard not derivable
- **Restringing** (Lupita): ~40h (5 days); complex/grand up to 110h
- **DHRT**: specialist Garrett Taylor ~66h; senior Jake ~24h; trainees
  124–208h; Curtis final voicing 1.5h/piano
- **In-store tunings**: 1.5h each, ~2 per piano (4–6 for full restos) — all
  on Korban's calendar (chip tuning never appears as a term; map it there)
- **Refinishing** (Doris, 10h blocks): full refinish ~60h; touch-up ~10h;
  some outsourced to "Vega Refinish" (calendar under-counts)
- **Key service** (Marcelo): batch queue, per-piano not derivable from
  titles; full ivory job (Victoria) 112h
- **QC/assembly**: simple pre-delivery (Jake) 2.5h; full assembly+QC
  (McKinly) ~17h (up to 49h)
- **Refurb checklist** (Jacob): ~48h; grand ~64h vs upright ~42h
- **Plate hardware buffing** (Korban): 4h blocks, generic queue
- **Player restoration**: 250–450h (Jake 248h, Matthew 433h)
- New arrival ~2–3h per container event; assessment/exit-prep never
  calendared as phases
