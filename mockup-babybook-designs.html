Three designs for Myles's birth story

Each one is set in Abby's nursery palette of ivory, sage and tan. The pages below are letter-size previews using the real timeline from October 10, 2026. Photo frames are placeholders until the album is linked.

A · Linen & Sage

An heirloom keepsake. Centered classic typography, a monogram cover, thin tan rules and a sage double frame. Reads like a book that gets passed down.

Feel

Quiet, timeless, formal. The least "trendy" of the three, which means it will look right in twenty years.

Best for

A hardcover photo book (Artifact Uprising, Mixbook) and a framed first page in the nursery.

B · Arch & Bloom

Modern nursery. Arch-shaped photo windows, soft sage color bands, rounded tan tiles for the facts, and big friendly numerals for the moments that matter.

Feel

Warm, current, a little playful. Matches the arch-and-sage nursery look that's everywhere right now.

Best for

A softcover layflat book and the web version, since the cards and tiles translate directly to a phone screen.

C · Grandma's Journal

A scrapbook in Karmel's own voice. Ruled paper, Polaroid frames with washi tape, a stamped room number, and handwritten margin notes next to the typed record.

Feel

Personal, tactile, funny. Leans into the fact that this was witnessed and written by Grandma.

Best for

A spiral or linen journal print, and it's the easiest for Abby to keep adding to, since a new entry is just another note and Polaroid.

How the print book and the web app fit together. Whichever design you pick becomes one page template. The story lives as a list of chapters and entries (time, text, photos). The web version renders that list on a phone and lets Abby add a chapter or an entry with photos. The print version renders the same list as letter-size pages with page breaks, ready to save as a PDF and upload to a photo-book printer.
